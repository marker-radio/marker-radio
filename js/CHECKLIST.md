# Marker Radio — Story Rules

Not a marker reader. A ride-along storyteller.

When a drive through a town ends, you should know how it exists, what shaped it, and who lived there.

A city is done when all seven are built. Then lock it.
The only holes left: empty `long` text, and `names` for later “tell me more.”

1. Landmarks — historical society sites, plaques, museums, courthouses, marked buildings.
2. How the town was formed — railroads, rivers, canals, early roads, industry.
3. The people — born, lived, died, buried. Inventors, presidents, athletes, names the town still says.
4. Parks and public land — national, state, reservoirs, wildlife areas, squares you drive past.
5. Native history — villages, portages, trails, treaty grounds. Sourced, not filed as legend.
6. War and freedom — battlefields, forts, camps, depots, Underground Railroad.
7. Local legends — past and present. Frame as legend.

Card shape for every story:
id, name, lat, lng, radius, short, long (empty ok), names (hooks for later).
