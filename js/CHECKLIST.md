# Marker Radio — Story Rules

Every city must include all five categories before it's considered done.

1. **Landmarks** — every marked historic site, historic building, museum, fort, or society building you can drive past.
2. **Railroads and canals** — lines, depots, shops, towpaths. These explain why the town exists.
3. **Local legends** — folklore, ghosts, "people say." Frame as legend: "Local story says…"
4. **Famous people** — born, lived, worked, died, or buried there. Includes graves.
5. **National parks** — if any fall inside the area.

Source of truth for landmarks: hmdb.org plus the state historical marker list. Search before writing a city
